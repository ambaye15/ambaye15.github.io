PRISM uses masked optical flow to characterize
stereotypical motor movements from video. It measures four aspects of
movement: intensity, extent, frequency, and rhythmicity. These measurements
allow us to compare movement bouts within and across individuals and to
identify bouts that are similar in one respect but differ in another.
