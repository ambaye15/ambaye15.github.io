**Chiral Multiparameter Persistence (CaMPers)** uses topology to study how handed interactions organize across spatial scales. 
It can distinguish patterns that have the same vorticity measurements but differ in how rotation is arranged in space. 
The project develops mathematical and computational tools for measuring this organization, with applications to simulated flows and embryonic development.
