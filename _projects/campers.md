CaMPers is a framework for quantifying how same-handed interactions are
organized across spatial scales in sampled vector fields. It builds separate
directed complexes for positive and negative rotation, then uses Euler
characteristic curves to summarize their organization. The framework also
shows where contributions to these summaries arise in the field. We use it
to study chirality in synthetic flows and embryonic tissue.
