Research

My work lies at the intersection of topology, geometry, and data science, with a focus on developing topological methods for analyzing time-evolving systems. I am especially interested in applications to biological, medical, and neuroscientific data, where complex dynamics arise across multiple spatial and temporal scales. My research combines persistent homology, geometric learning, and computational modeling to reveal structure, organization, and dynamics in real-world systems.
