I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Northeastern University, advised by Jose Perea. I hold a B.A. in Mathematics from Vassar College with a minor in Education.

I develop mathematical and computational methods for extracting structure from complex data. My work draws on applied topology, geometry, machine learning, and signal processing, with applications in computational biology, medical imaging, and the analysis of dynamical systems. For more, see my CV, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Google Scholar.
